# Privacy Policy - This or That

**Last Updated: August 3, 2026**

## 1. Introduction

Welcome to This or That.

Your privacy is important to us. This Privacy Policy explains how This or That handles your information when you use the application on iOS and Android devices.

## 2. Data Collection

This or That does **not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal information**.

Specifically, the application does not collect:

- Name
- Email address
- Phone number
- Location
- Contacts
- Photos
- Camera data
- Microphone data
- Device identifiers
- Advertising identifiers (IDFA or AAID)
- Analytics data
- Crash reports linked to you
- Account information

The application does not require creating an account.

## 3. Third-Party Services

This or That does not use third-party services that collect personal data.

## 4. Advertising

This or That does not display personalized advertisements.

If this changes in the future, this Privacy Policy will be updated before any data collection begins.

## 5. Children's Privacy

This or That does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

## 6. Data Security

Since no personal data is collected or stored, there is no personal information retained on our servers.

## 7. Your Rights

Because we do not collect any personal information, there is no personal data to access, modify, export, or delete.

## 8. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time.

Any changes will be reflected by updating the "Last Updated" date above.
