# Privacy Policy - 5 Words

**Last Updated: February 22, 2026**

## 1. Introduction

At 5 Words, we prioritize the protection of your personal data. This privacy policy describes how we collect, use, store, and protect your information when you use our application on Apple devices.

## 2. Data Collected

### 2.1 User-Provided Data
- **Authentication**: Unique identifier (UUID), username, email address
- **Profile**: Profile photo (optional), language preferences
- **Game**: Match results, scores, game statistics

### 2.2 Automatically Collected Data
- **Device Data**: Device type, iOS version, advertising identifier (IDFA)
- **Usage Data**: Actions within the application, session duration, features used
- **Location Data**: Approximate position (with permission)
- **Technical Data**: IP address, timezone, system language

### 2.3 Cookies and Similar Technologies
We use tracking technologies to analyze app usage and improve your experience.

## 3. Data Usage

We use your data for:

- **Service Provision**: App operation, authentication, data synchronization
- **Improvement**: Performance analysis, feature optimization
- **Communication**: Push notifications, customer support, important announcements
- **Analytics**: Aggregated statistics, usage trends
- **Advertising**: Personalized advertising content (with consent)
- **Compliance**: Legal obligations

## 4. Data Sharing

### 4.1 Authorized Third Parties
We share your data with:

- **Service Providers**: Cloud hosting, analytics (Firebase, Mixpanel)
- **Apple Partners**: In compliance with App Store guidelines
- **Technical Providers**: Infrastructure, security, support

### 4.2 Restrictions
We never sell your personal data. Sharing is limited to the purposes described in this policy.

## 5. Data Security

### 5.1 Protection Measures
- **Encryption**: TLS 1.3 for data in transit
- **Authentication**: OAuth 2.0 and Apple Sign In protocols
- **Storage**: Encryption of sensitive data at rest
- **Access**: Role-based access restrictions

### 5.2 Limitations
While we implement robust measures, no system is 100% secure.

## 6. Your Rights

In accordance with applicable laws (GDPR, CCPA, etc.), you can:

- **Access** your personal data
- **Correct** inaccurate information
- **Delete** your data (with certain legal exceptions)
- **Export** your data in a structured format
- **Withdraw** your consent at any time
- **Object** to marketing processing

To exercise these rights, contact us at: `privacy@5words.app`

## 7. Data Retention

- **Active Account**: Data retained as long as you use the service
- **After Deletion**: Deletion within 30 days (except legal obligations)
- **Analytics Data**: Aggregated and retained 12-24 months

## 8. Apple Sign In Integration

When you use **Apple Sign In**:

- We receive only the unique identifier provided by Apple
- Apple does not share your email without explicit consent
- You maintain full control of your Apple account
- You can revoke access via Settings > [Your Name] > Password & Security

## 9. Location and Regional Data

### 9.1 European Union (GDPR)
- Legal basis: Consent or legitimate interest
- Right to be forgotten: Fully respected
- Data Protection Officer: Available upon request

### 9.2 California (CCPA)
- Right to know: Full access to your data
- Right to deletion: Requests processed within 45 days
- Right to opt-out of sale: Your data is never sold

## 10. Push Notifications

We send notifications for:
- Match invitations
- Match results
- Important updates

**You can disable** notifications via Settings > 5 Words > Notifications

## 11. Policy Changes

We may update this policy. Changes will be:
- Notified by email for significant changes
- Displayed in the application
- Effective 30 days after notification

Your continued use of the application after changes constitutes your acceptance.

## 12. Contact and Complaints

- **Email**: privacy@5words.app

- **Response Time**: Maximum 30 days

### Complaints

## 13. Additional iOS Information

### App Tracking Transparency (ATT)
- We display a permission request for advertising tracking
- Your choice is respected and can be modified in Settings > Privacy > Tracking

### Sandboxing
The application operates in a secure environment (sandbox) that limits access to system data.

### Password Verification Codes
If you use SMS verification codes, we do not retain them after verification.

## 14. Minor Consent

- Minimum age to create an account: 13 years (or applicable age in your jurisdiction)
- For minors: Parent/legal guardian consent required
- No direct marketing deliberately targeting minors

## 15. Glossary

- **Personal Data**: Any information identifying an individual
- **Processing**: Collection, use, storage, sharing of data
- **Consent**: Voluntary and explicit agreement
- **Legitimate Interest**: Valid business reason for processing data
